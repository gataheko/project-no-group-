# Project Proposal: Small Business Inventory System

## Problem
Small shops can lose track of stock, overlook products that need reordering, and struggle to estimate the value of goods on hand.

## Proposed solution
A local web application written in C# with ASP.NET Core MVC and SQLite. Staff can review inventory, add or update products, search the catalog, monitor low stock, and view simple reports.

## Primary users
- Small business owner
- Inventory clerk

## Scope
- Product catalog and inventory quantity tracking
- Category and supplier references
- Search, sort, and low-stock filtering
- Inventory valuation reports
- Optional barcode product prefill through Open Food Facts

## Limitations
This starter version does not include authentication, multi-user permissions, sales transactions, purchase orders, or editable category/supplier forms.
